Why we built Alfred

A dozen tabs, and the move was already gone.

We trade options every day. For years our process was a dozen tabs: charts in one, the option chain in another, options flow in a third, news and X in the rest. By the time everything lined up, the move had already started.

Most tools assume you already know how to read all of that. Signal groups skip the reading entirely and hand you someone else's entry, with no reason and no record of the losses. Neither one helps a developing trader get better.

So we built the desk we wanted. Alfred's agents read the charts, the chain, options flow, dealer positioning and the news, then explain each setup in plain English: what supports it, what would prove it wrong, and when there is no trade. We have traded our own money from it since May 2026 and opened it to members in July 2026.

How we build it

Three rules we do not bend.

01

Explain before you act.

Every read comes with its evidence and the condition that would change it. If Alfred cannot explain a setup, it does not show one.

02

Keep the whole record.

Omega's picks are hashed at 10:05 AM ET before outcomes are known, and every result stays on the public record, losses included.

03

You make the call.

Alfred is read-only. It never places trades or manages money. Our job is research and education. The decision is always yours.
