Partner rules

Read these before you post.

  1. Disclose the relationship. Say you earn a commission in the post itself, next to the recommendation and before the link, as FTC guidelines require.
  2. No performance promises. Do not promise returns or present any result as typical. You can link the public record.
  3. Education, not advice. Do not call Alfred a signal service or say it tells people what to buy or sell.
  4. Your own channels only. No paid search ads on Alfred, A&O or tradewithao brand terms, no coupon or deal sites, and no unsolicited bulk messages.
  5. Use our assets, do not copy the product. Use the images and videos we give partners. Do not copy, record or republish Alfred's interface, research or content beyond them.
  6. No self-referrals. Your own membership earns nothing. Refunded or disputed payments reverse their commission. We may end a partnership that breaks these rules.
