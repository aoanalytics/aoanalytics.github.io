How to hunt stocks without a signal Discord

If you already pay a chat for tickers, you are buying names. Hunt is the map those names were supposed to come with. Same 1–5 for a first tape and a tenth. AO only publishes a stock plan on 5/5 Breakout and 1/5 Breakdown. Waiting is the filter. Education, not advice.

CHAT

Someone drops a ticker.

You pay monthly for screenshots and a list. The score, if there is one, is whoever posted it. A new trader and a tenth-year desk get the same ping. There is no shared invalidation language. There is no Waiting filter. There is only speed and FOMO.

DESK

Hunt, then the 1–5.

Location is not a score. The heatmap is delayed. Live 1–5 is the desk. 5/5 Breakout or 1/5 Breakdown gets a plan. 2, 3, and 4 wait. Flow In Depth is the print after, not a second algo. You do not need someone else’s book to know what your book is doing.

How to use Hunt

  1. Members: open desk with credentials. Hunt is on the member desk, not a public board.
  2. Read the 1–5. It does not get easier because you are new.
  3. Take only 5/5 Breakout and 1/5 Breakdown. Waiting is the filter.
  4. Screenshot the Hunt board. Caption is four short lines. Then open Flow In Depth for the print when size qualifies.
  5. If flow, magnets, and the 1–5 disagree, do nothing. Waiting is a read, not a miss.

Simple Mode keeps Hunt readable for the first pass. Pro Mode adds depth when you want more context around the same score. The score itself does not soften for a new login.

What people get wrong about Hunt

  • They treat the heatmap like a buy list. It is not. Location tells you where attention is. The 1–5 tells you whether AO will even publish a plan.
  • They treat a 3 like almost a 5. It is not. 2, 3, and 4 wait. Plans only on 5/5 Breakout and 1/5 Breakdown.
  • They treat Flow In Depth like a second score. It is not. It unmasks a print. It does not raise a 3 to a 5.
  • They chase random tickers because Hunt looked busy. Customized watchlist first. YOUR names across 0DTE, weeklies, monthlies, and LEAPS.

FAQ

What is Hunt?

Hunt is the first map on the member desk. It shows where the tape is hot. Location is not a score. The live 1–5 is the desk.

Why only 5/5 and 1/5 get plans?

Because Waiting is the filter. AO only publishes a stock plan on 5/5 Breakout and 1/5 Breakdown. Everything else waits so the board does not become another signal dump.

Does a new account get a softer 1–5?

No. Same 1–5 for a first tape and a tenth.

Can I use Hunt without paying for a signal Discord?

Yes. That is the point. Hunt replaces the need to buy names from a chat. Start with the $1 for a 30-day desk trial, then $50/month Desk if you keep it. Free Discord is optional and secondary.

What should my Hunt screenshot caption include?

Four short lines: ticker, 1–5, why it ranks or why it waits, and what you will check next (usually Flow In Depth or invalidation). Keep it desk language, not hype.

Does flow change Hunt?

Flow can add context after a qualifying print. It does not override the 1–5. Agreement matters. Disagreement means wait.
