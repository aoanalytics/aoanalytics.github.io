How to hunt stocks without a signal Discord

If you already pay a chat for tickers, you are buying names. Hunt is the map those names were supposed to come with. Same 1–5 for a first tape and a tenth. AO only publishes a stock plan on 5/5 Breakout and 1/5 Breakdown. Waiting is the filter.

CHAT

Someone drops a ticker.

You pay monthly for screenshots and a list. The score, if there is one, is whoever posted it. A new trader and a tenth-year desk get the same ping.

DESK

Hunt, then the 1–5.

Location is not a score. The heatmap is delayed. Live 1–5 is the desk. 5/5 Breakout or 1/5 Breakdown gets a plan. 2, 3, and 4 wait. Flow In Depth is the print after, not a second algo.

How to use Hunt

  1. Members: open desk with credentials. Hunt is on the member desk, not a public board.
  2. Read the 1–5. It does not get easier because you are new.
  3. Take only 5/5 Breakout and 1/5 Breakdown. Waiting is the filter.
  4. Screenshot the Hunt board. Caption is four lines. Then Flow In Depth for the print.
