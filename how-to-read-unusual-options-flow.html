PROMPT-SHAPED LESSON

How to read unusual options flow without treating premium as a signal

A $26M call print looks like a buy. Most of the time it is a headline. The desk is whether both legs printed, whether the size is opening, whether it agrees with the 1–5, and where it sits versus the walls. AO Flow In Depth is that unmask on one card. Screenshot the card. Do not dump the table.

HEADLINE

Wow. Size printed.

Premium, side, strike. Super bullish or super bearish, right? That is what a signal chat posts. It is also what you see if you only screenshot the notional.

DESK

Well, not exactly.

Both legs? Closing or rolling? Fights the 1–5. Intrinsic vs time. Put wall and call wall. If those disagree, waiting is the filter. Flow never raises a 3 to a 5.

What AO will not invent

Chain snapshots do not reconstruct a named structure. Do not call a print a collar, a calendar, or a roll unless the tape actually shows it. One qualifying print. Full context on the card. Caption is four beats: wow, well not exactly, the desk sentence, then Hunt.

How to use it on the desk

  1. Hunt first. Same 1–5 as every other screen.
  2. Open Flow In Depth on that ticker. One card, 720px, Tesla-black.
  3. If no qualifying print, do not post a fake card. Waiting is the read.
  4. Copy the short X draft under the screenshot. One print per post.
