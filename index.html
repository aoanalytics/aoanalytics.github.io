Questions worth asking

The details matter.

Is Alfred an automated trading bot?

No. The customer-facing Alfred desk is built around research and human decisions. It does not place brokerage orders for retail customers. Internal Omega paper research is separate from this product and should not be confused with customer execution.

What does a contract-fit score mean?

It organizes how the displayed contract compares with the stated research and filtering criteria. It does not mean an 88% chance of profit and is not a prediction of an investment outcome. Always review liquidity, IV, underlying price movement and potential loss.

Can I use Alfred to research stocks and longer-term strategies?

Yes. The existing desk includes stock and options research, and A&O is developing a more integrated Investing & Income workflow. Feature availability depends on the released build; illustrations on this page are clearly identified as concepts.

Does a large options print tell us what a trader expects?

Not necessarily. A trade may be an opening or closing transaction, a hedge, or one leg of a spread. Print-side estimates and open-interest figures have important limitations. Alfred aims to make these visible rather than present every large call print as a bullish bet.

What is the difference between Simple and Pro?

Simple emphasizes the familiar setup tiles, watchlists and quick summaries. Pro is designed to expose deeper contract research, charts, flow, dealer levels and evidence. The two experiences should rely on the same authoritative research data.
