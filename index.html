The A&O trading workflow.

Simple enough to use live. Strict enough to keep you from forcing bad setups.

1

Read the tape

Start with the broader index environment, news risk, and whether market internals are confirming the move.

2

Check dealer pressure

Compare price to yFLIP, high-volume walls, GEX/DEX pressure, and whether dealers are likely supporting or resisting price.

3

Grade momentum

Use score, volume, trend, VWAP, RSI, relative strength, and location to decide whether the setup deserves attention.

4

Choose the contract

Favor strikes that fit the thesis, premium range, expiry window, Greeks, liquidity, and risk-reward plan.

Quick answers.

Is this financial advice?

No. A&O Analytics is educational market commentary and community discussion. Every trader is responsible for their own risk.

What markets does the community focus on?

The main focus is U.S. options flow and index trading, especially SPX, SPY, QQQ, NDX, and IWM, with selected momentum names on the watchlist.

Do I need the Signal Terminal?

No. The Discord is useful on its own, but the Signal Terminal gives members a clearer framework for the levels, scores, and dealer positioning being discussed.
