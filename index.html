Why we skip contracts under about $1.00 to $1.50

Very cheap contracts are usually far out of the money. Their low delta means they barely move with the stock, time decay takes a bigger share of their value every day, and they need a large move, fast, to pay off before expiration. Big moves are the exception, not the rule, so A&O does not build around them.

Questions

Frequently asked questions.

What is A&O?

A customizable options and investing terminal. You set your plan: tickers, strategies from 0DTE to LEAPS, spreads, premium selling or shares, the price ranges you like to trade and your risk limits. A&O's agents scan for setups that fit, explain the structure behind them and let you backtest rules before you trade.

Is this personalized investment advice?

No. A&O is a research tool with filters you control. It shows which setups match the rules you wrote. It does not know your full financial situation, does not tell you what to buy or sell, and does not place trades. Every decision is yours.

How is A&O different from a signal Discord?

A signal room picks tickers for you, pushes alerts and sells cheap contracts as lottery tickets. A&O has no alerts and no picks. You set the tickers and strategies, and the desk shows what fits, with the levels and the invalidation. Read the comparison: AO vs paying for a signal Discord.

Can I backtest a strategy?

Yes. Rule Lab replays a plain rule on up to five years of daily history and shows what price did after each time it fired. The Strategy League paper-trades rules live. Past results do not predict future ones.

Can I connect my broker?

Read-only broker connections through broker-approved sign-in are rolling out. A&O never sees your broker password and never places orders.

What does the $1 trial include?

Thirty days of the full terminal so you can set your plan, run the scan, backtest rules and track your portfolio before the $50/month Desk continues.
