House rules

Waiting is a position.

The desk is built to keep you out of bad trades as much as to find good ones.

No first-15 entries

IV is wild from 9:30 to 9:45. The desk pauses and tells you when reads unlock.

No edge is an answer

When the read is mid-range, the tile says so and tells you what would change it.

Flow never overrides structure

A big print is evidence. It does not turn a wait into a buy.

Questions

Straight answers.

What is AO?

An options analytics desk. It reads dealer positioning, options flow and the live tape on your tickers, then gives each one a model score and a plain-English read: lean calls, lean puts, or wait.

Is this financial advice?

No. AO is educational analytics. Nothing here is a recommendation to buy or sell. Options involve substantial risk. The model score measures how well a setup fits AO's rules, not the odds of profit.

Does AO place trades for me?

No. AO never touches your brokerage account and never places trades. You decide, you verify prices with your broker, and you manage your own risk.

Why not just join an alert room?

An alert is someone else's read, delivered after they took it. AO shows you the same dealer levels, flow and tape they look at, on your tickers, while it is happening. You keep the decision.
