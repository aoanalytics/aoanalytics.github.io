Keep your watchlist, preferred strategies, premium range and time horizon together. Compare setups against your budget and risk settings before making a decision.

Market context in one view.

Review index setups, model scores, dealer levels, your watchlist and upcoming earnings and economic events without losing the wider market context.

  • Upcoming earnings and the options-implied move
  • Dealer-level context as prices change
  • Updated research throughout the session
03 · Any ticker

From a ticker to the details.

Explore dealer levels, a plain-English market read and ranked options contracts in one ticker view. Use the ranking to organize your research, not as a probability of profit.

  • In, at and out of the money, per style
  • Contract fit checked against your plan
  • Review the rationale, then inspect the details
04 · Rule Lab

Test it before you trade it.

Choose a ticker and a rule, then explore what the underlying price did after historical matches. Rule Lab shows up to five years of daily history and outcomes 5, 10 and 20 sessions later.

  • Searchable rules: moving averages, RSI, gaps, highs
  • Results plotted with small samples called out
  • Export to CSV
05 · Morning Brief

Start with the bigger picture.

Review overnight developments, futures, volatility, key dealer levels and notable movers before building your own view of the session.

  • Overnight gaps and opening context
  • Earnings and macro for the week
  • Context for the holdings you follow
06 · Flow

Options activity, with context.

Examine notable options prints, trade-side estimates and possible multi-leg activity. A large print alone does not establish the trader's intent or predict the stock's next move.

  • Call and put premium activity by ticker
  • Next-day open-interest context
  • Search any ticker
07 · Strategy League

Observe strategies without real capital.

Follow Rule Lab strategies in a paper environment and compare their recorded results. Paper outcomes are hypothetical and do not represent what a live account would necessarily earn.

  • Review both winning and losing results

Following a trade callout

  • Start with another person's trade idea
  • Inherit their ticker, strike and expiry
  • Work out whether it fits your own budget
  • Research the thesis and exit conditions yourself
  • Evaluate the evidence behind any performance claim

Researching with A&O

  • Start with your tickers, premiums, time horizon and risk criteria
  • Compare ranked contracts and strategy choices
  • Review the rationale and what could challenge it
  • Keep market context and changing conditions in view
  • Explore backtests and paper records, including losses
!

Premium is a price, not a risk score.

A lower premium does not automatically make an option safer, and a higher premium does not make it better. Consider the strike, expiration, liquidity, volatility and strategy together. Risk depends on the full position; some option-writing strategies can lose more than the premium received.

Education, not advice. Options involve substantial risk and are not suitable for every investor.

Questions

Frequently asked questions.

What is A&O?

A&O is an AI-assisted research platform for retail options traders and stock investors. Set your own watchlist, premium range, strategies, time horizon and risk criteria, then explore market context, ranked contracts, portfolio research and historical testing. It is research and decision support, not a signal service.

Is this personalized investment advice?

No. A&O is a research tool with filters you control. It shows which setups match the rules you wrote. It does not know your full financial situation, does not tell you what to buy or sell, and does not place trades. Every decision is yours.

How is A&O different from a signal Discord?

A trade callout starts with someone else's idea. A&O starts with your criteria and gives you tools to research the evidence, compare contracts and test rules. You decide whether an idea fits your own plan. Read the comparison: AO vs paying for a signal Discord.

Can I backtest a strategy?

Yes. Rule Lab tests rules against up to five years of daily price history and shows the underlying's subsequent behavior. Those price outcomes should not be read as actual options fills or realized P&L. Strategy League provides separate paper-strategy results. Neither guarantees future performance.

Can I connect my broker?

Supported brokerages can connect read-only through SnapTrade so you can research your positions in My Portfolio. Review the permissions before connecting. A&O does not take custody of your assets or submit orders.

Where can I review model results?

Visit the public paper-trade record and read the methodology alongside the outcomes. Model paper results are hypothetical, not actual customer returns, and may differ from live execution.

Is there a guide to the terminal?

Yes. The member guide (PDF) walks through Your Plan, the setup cards, dealer levels, Ask A&O, Rule Lab, Alpha Flow and the rest of the desk, with real screens.

What does the $1 trial include?

Thirty days of the full terminal so you can set your plan, run the scan, backtest rules and track your portfolio before the $50/month Desk continues.
