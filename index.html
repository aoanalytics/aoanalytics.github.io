QUESTIONS, ANSWERED

Frequently asked
questions.

Clear plan boundaries, responsible-trading expectations, and secure demo access before joining.

What markets does A&O cover?+

AO focuses on SPX, SPY, QQQ, NDX, and IWM for intraday options context, plus selected momentum names and weekly swing candidates.

What is included in A&O Free?+

A&O Free includes the free Discord, one daily 0DTE trade idea, and two weekly swing trade ideas.

How is AO different from paying for a signal Discord?+

A signal chat sells names. AO is the desk: Hunt for where the tape is hot, then the same 1–5, Flow In Depth, and sticky vs ice gamma. AO does not place trades. Read the comparison: AO vs paying for a signal Discord.

What comes with the $29.99 AO Bot plan?+

AO Bot is the customized Discord bot on your tickers and risk. One month free, then month two at 50% off after a short feedback form. Founder promo members already billed at $9.99 keep that rate.

What is the difference between Swing Desk and Full Desk?+

Swing Desk ($69.99) is the customized 0DTE bot plus the dashboard for weeklies, monthlies, LEAPS, dealer positioning, and options flow. Full Desk ($99.99) adds 0DTE on the dashboard and agents that help you improve trades. Bring-your-own API is not in this launch.

Where do I reset my password?+

There is no separate A&O password. Whop is the account. Reset the password on Whop, then keep Discord linked so the bot and paid rooms stay attached to the same seat.

How often are trade ideas shared?+

Ideas are shared when qualified setups appear—not on a forced schedule. AO is designed around quality, confirmation, and a defined invalidation rather than constant callouts.

Does A&O teach risk management?+

Yes. The workflow emphasizes defined entries, targets, stops, invalidation, position awareness, and pausing or reducing size when a plan is no longer valid.

Does A&O place trades for me?+

No. AO provides educational analytics and decision-support tools. You remain responsible for verification, risk, broker actions, and every trading decision.

How do I hunt stocks without a signal Discord?+

Open Hunt. Same 1–5 for a first tape and a tenth. AO only publishes a stock plan on 5/5 Breakout and 1/5 Breakdown. Waiting is the filter.

Can I see the dashboard before joining?+

Yes. Open Hunt is the public desk — no password. Dashboard Login is the private terminal after you have a profile. The marketing site never collects credentials.
