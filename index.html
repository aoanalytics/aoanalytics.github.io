QUESTIONS, ANSWERED

Frequently asked
questions.

Clear plan boundaries, responsible-trading expectations, and secure demo access before joining.

What markets does A&O cover?+

AO focuses on SPX, SPY, QQQ, NDX, and IWM for intraday options context, plus selected momentum names and weekly swing candidates.

What is included in A&O Free?+

A&O Free includes access to the free Discord, one 0DTE trade idea, and one weekly swing trade idea.

What comes with the $9.99 Quant Bot plan?+

The A&O Quant Bot plan includes the Quant Bot, premium trade ideas, detailed chart analysis, Dealer Matrix context, and Options Flow. It starts with a seven-day free trial.

What does the $19.99 Dashboard Beta include?+

Dashboard Beta includes Pro and Simple Mode, a personalized Quant Bot, Trade Life Agents, bring-your-own API support, and agentic orchestration.

How often are trade ideas shared?+

Ideas are shared when qualified setups appear—not on a forced schedule. AO is designed around quality, confirmation, and a defined invalidation rather than constant callouts.

Does A&O teach risk management?+

Yes. The workflow emphasizes defined entries, targets, stops, invalidation, position awareness, and pausing or reducing size when a plan is no longer valid.

Does A&O place trades for me?+

No. AO provides educational analytics and decision-support tools. You remain responsible for verification, risk, broker actions, and every trading decision.

Can I see the dashboard before joining?+

Yes. Demo Login opens the protected AO Signal Terminal. Your browser will ask for the A&O demo username and password provided to you separately; the marketing site never collects those credentials.
