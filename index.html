Why a desk

Signal rooms sell you a ping.

Most paid rooms run the same morning. The call lands, everyone piles in, and you are the last fill with no plan for what comes next.

  1. 9:31 ETThe alert fires.Implied volatility is still inflated from the open.
  2. 9:33 ETYou chase the fill.The contract is already up. You pay the worst price of the morning.
  3. 9:52 ETIt reverses.No level, no stop, no idea why it moved. You wait for the next ping.

A&O gives you the desk instead.

  • The level, the flow and the reason on one screen, before the bell.

Questions

Before you start.

Is this a signal service?

No. A&O does not send buy or sell alerts. The desk shows you the levels, the flow and the reason for each setup, filtered to your plan, and you decide. It is an education tool, not financial advice.

Does A&O place trades or hold my money?

No. You trade in your own brokerage account. If you connect a broker, the connection is read only, so you can see your positions next to the desk.

Where does the data come from?

Live options chains and greeks from Schwab market data, scanned through the session. Dealer levels and flow are computed from that data on our servers.

I am new to options. Is it too much?

Simple mode shows one answer per ticker in plain English. Your Plan keeps you inside a budget and a price range, and the Learn hub explains every term on the screen.
