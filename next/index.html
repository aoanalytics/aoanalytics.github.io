Trend, VWAP, moving averages and volume, plus today's breakout levels, with how often price reached the next level in past cases, counted and shown.

Whether premium is cheap or rich against how much the stock actually moves, and which contract gives the most for your budget, with every number side by side.

Does Alfred trade for me?

No. Alfred is research. You decide and you place every trade at your own broker. Omega, our internal paper-trading agent, tests our models separately and in public.

What does a score mean?

How well a contract lines up with your choices and our criteria. It is not a chance of profit.

Where do the odds come from?

From what happened in past cases on our own data, with the number of cases shown next to every figure. When there are too few, Alfred says so.
