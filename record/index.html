Live testing: these agents are being built in the open

Omega and the signal engine are paper-trading agents under active development. We are traders first, and we change the rules when the record shows us something is off. What we are building toward:

  • One clear exit rule: from Oct 22, Omega takes profit at +30% and stops at −50%, chosen by testing every exit rule on its own trades (below).
  • Adaptive take profit, stop loss and trailing, sized from the market, in test lanes beside the official rule; one joins only if it beats it.
  • Trading it as a book: per-trade and open-risk limits by account size, a daily profit goal that banks the day, and a daily portfolio review, so results come from a system rather than single bets.

Built on retail data. A&O runs on a standard broker data feed and free public sources, sampled every few seconds to a few minutes, not an institutional tick-by-tick feed. Signals and prices are what A&O saw at the time, so they can trail what a desk with direct exchange feeds sees. Any edge here comes from the model, not from paying for speed.

Every change is dated on this page. No result is edited, backfilled or removed: each trade keeps the rule it was recorded under, and corrections are new dated entries. Paper results only, not real fills. Education only, not financial advice.

Which exit rule wins

On Omega’s 64 trades, holding at +30% up / −50% down would have won 59.4% of the time, versus 53.1% as actually traded. Holding to the end wins less often (34.6% of the 26 that have expired), but the winners are huge. From Oct 22, +30% / −50% is Omega’s official exit rule.

What we changed, and why

We trade these setups ourselves. By Oct 21 the record matched what we saw on our own screens: on choppy days the read flipped between calls and puts every time price crossed VWAP (SPY crossed it 7 times on Oct 21), signals were being scored on candles that had not finished, the first half hour produced mostly puts with no better hit rate, and fixed +30% / −30% exits sold winners early and rode losers to the stop. These are the fixes. They apply to new trades from Oct 22; every earlier trade stays under the rule it was recorded with.
