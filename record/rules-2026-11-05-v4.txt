A&O Omega official rules, frozen Oct 26 to Dec 4, 2026. Picks: Omega looks every 5 minutes from 9:45 AM to 3 PM ET, at most 4 picks a day (2 on a chop day), no new 0DTE after 1:30 PM. A pick needs a read that clears the desk's bar and agrees with the SPY and QQQ read; no new call when the chart trends or leans down, no new put when it trends or leans up. Entry checks: a 1/5 or 5/5 with a location score under 40, or with no valid location score, is not taken; at most one pick per index group (S&P 500, Nasdaq 100, Russell 2000, Dow); a pick whose option money is 2x against it is skipped unless its location is 60 or more; an index pick waits while the day's range is tight and the chart is not trending. Contracts: bid/ask spread 5% or less (4% for 0DTE and 1DTE, 8% for LEAPS), 500+ open interest. Exits: +30% target and -50% stop on the option, nothing else; 0DTE closes at 3:45 PM, 1DTE at 3:45 PM the next session, everything else at +30%, -50% or expiry. Results at the mid, with an after-cost estimate beside them. Every pick is kept. Amended Nov 2, 2026 (version 3, from the first look after it went live that day): a pick is not taken when the desk capped its read because the move was stretched, or when the Reversal agent called a turn the other way on that name in the last 45 minutes (a watch or a confirmed call that has not failed). Guarded picks are followed on paper in a test lane, apart from Omega's record. Amended Nov 5, 2026 (version 4, from the first look after it went live that day): an index pick (S&P 500, Nasdaq 100, Russell 2000 or Dow group) on a 4/5 or 2/5 read is not taken when the day's price has tagged the other side's Golden Gate (yesterday's close plus or minus 0.382 times the daily 14-day ATR) and not its own, or before either side has tagged it. 5/5 and 1/5 reads and stock picks are not affected. Skipped picks are followed on paper in the guard lane.
